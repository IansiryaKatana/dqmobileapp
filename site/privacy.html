Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Privacy Terms Support

This policy explains how Mercy 4 All Foundation (“we”, “us”, “our”), a registered charity in the United Kingdom, collects and uses information in the Donate Quran mobile app.

Donate Quran lets you donate toward Quran printing, order a free printed Quran, read and listen to the Quran, find the Qibla, learn (including wudu, prayer, Umrah and Hajj), and ask a scholar.

This policy matches the data practices declared on the App Store privacy labels. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Controller: Mercy 4 All Foundation (Donate Quran project)
Website: https://donatequran.com
Support: https://donatequran.com/contact
Email: support@donatequran.com

2. You can use the app as a guest

You do not need an account for core features (donate, order a free Quran, read, Qibla, learn, Ask a Scholar).

If you create an account, we can show your donation receipts, order history, saved addresses, and scholar questions across devices.

3. Information we collect

We (and our processors listed below) collect the following. “Collect” means information is sent off your device and stored so we can run the app.

Account (optional)

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User ID (your account identifier)

Collected when you sign up, sign in, or update your profile.

Donations

  • Purchase history: amount, one-time or monthly, status, and receipt identifiers
  • Optional “on behalf of” or donor name you type (treated as other user content)

Donations are paid through the App Store or Google Play (via RevenueCat). We never receive or store your card number, Apple Pay details, or Google Play payment details.

Monthly donations are subscriptions you manage in your Apple ID or Google Play account.

Quran orders

  • Physical address (street, city, postcode)
  • Language, quantity, and order reference
  • Postage payment identifiers (not card numbers)

Printed Qurans are free. Postage is paid with PayPal. You complete payment in PayPal’s checkout. We do not store your PayPal password or card numbers.

If you are signed in, you may save a delivery address for later orders.

Ask a Scholar

  • Name, email, topic, and your question (other user content)

Questions go to our team privately. They are not posted on a public feed.

Notifications (optional)

  • Device ID: a push notification token, stored with your account if you are signed in

Used for order updates, donation confirmations, and scholar replies. You can refuse or turn notifications off in device settings.

Location (optional)

  • Precise location, only if you allow it, to show Qibla direction and prayer times

Location is not saved on your profile. A short-lived request may be sent to a magnetic-declination service so the compass can use true north. You can refuse location or revoke it later in system settings.

Diagnostics

  • Crash data from Firebase Crashlytics in release builds (device/app information in crash logs)

We do not attach crash reports to your name. We use them only to keep the app stable.

On your device only (not collected by us)

The Qibla compass uses motion / magnetometer sensors on the device. Bookmarks and some preferences may be stored locally on the phone. On-device data is not a “collection” unless it is sent to our servers.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Phone numbers
  • Payment card numbers or bank details
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone recordings
  • Advertising identifiers for ads
  • Analytics of taps, screens, or in-app behaviour
  • Sensitive information as a distinct field (we do not ask you to declare religion, health, or similar categories)

We do not show ads and we do not use your data for tracking.

5. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • Create and manage your optional account
  • Process donations and send receipts (when email is configured)
  • Fulfil free Quran orders and postage
  • Answer Ask a Scholar questions
  • Send the optional notifications you allow
  • Show Qibla and prayer times when location is allowed
  • Keep the app secure and reliable (including crash diagnostics)
  • Meet accounting, charity, and legal duties

Legal bases (UK GDPR), as applicable: performing a contract (account, donations, orders); legitimate interests (security, crash repair, charity operations); consent (location, notifications, optional form fields); and legal obligation (records we must keep).

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with processors who help us run Donate Quran, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — account authentication and app database
  • RevenueCat — App Store / Play Store donation purchases
  • Apple / Google — in-app donation payments
  • PayPal — postage payments for physical delivery
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports
  • NOAA (or equivalent) — magnetic declination for the Qibla compass, when location is allowed

When you read, search, or listen to the Quran, your device requests text from alquran.cloud and recitation audio from EveryAyah. Those providers receive the technical request needed to return that content.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account profile: until you delete your account, or it is inactive and we no longer need it
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, delete your account, or notifications are disabled
  • Scholar questions: while we are answering them and for a reasonable follow-up period
  • Donation and order records: as long as needed for fulfilment, receipts, charity accounting, and law (we may keep anonymised amounts and counts after account deletion)
  • Crash logs: for a limited diagnostic period
  • Location: not stored on your profile

8. Security and international transfers

Application data is stored with Supabase using access controls (including row-level security). Donation payments are handled by Apple or Google. Postage payments are handled by PayPal.

Our processors may be outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards permitted under UK data protection law (such as the provider’s contractual and transfer terms).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UK/EEA), you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete data (including by deleting your account)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive a copy of data you provided (portability)
  • Withdraw consent (location, notifications) in device settings

Email support@donatequran.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account (you must be signed in).

Deletion removes your login and profile. Donation and order records may be kept in anonymised or limited form for accounting and fulfilment. Store subscriptions must be cancelled in your Apple ID or Google Play settings; deleting the app account does not cancel a store subscription by itself.

10. Children

Donate Quran is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The Last updated date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
