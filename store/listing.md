## Full Description

Donate Quran is your trusted companion for sharing the message of Islam worldwide.

**Donate** — Fund Quran printing with one-time or monthly donations. 100% of public donations go towards printing.

**Order** — Request free Quran copies for yourself or your community.

**Read** — Access the Quran with Arabic text and English translation, audio playback, and bookmarks.

**Qibla** — Find prayer direction with a beautiful compass and prayer time links.

**Learn** — Articles and guides for new Muslims, including wudu and prayer instructions.

**Ask a Scholar** — Submit questions about Islam with care and privacy.

Built with a premium, respectful design inspired by the Donate Quran brand.
