# Donate Quran — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 2026

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** name and email when you create an account.
- **Donation and order data:** amounts, receipts, delivery addresses.
- **Device information:** push notification tokens for alerts.
- **Usage data:** app interactions to improve our service.

## How We Use Your Information

- Process donations and Quran orders.
- Send order updates and donation receipts.
- Respond to scholar questions.
- Improve app functionality and security.

## Data Storage

Data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Payment processing is handled by Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Stripe — we do not store card details.

## Third-Party Services

- Supabase (authentication and database)
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
- RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

## Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting support@donatequran.org.
